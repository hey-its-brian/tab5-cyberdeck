## v1.5.0: a cleaner home, app toggles, wireless tools and SENTINEL

Everything from the v1.5 betas, in one release.

- **A cleaner home.** A live panel (a big clock and the date, then sections from your apps, WEATHER today) and six fixed tiles: TERMINAL, NOTES, SETTINGS, CALC, FILES and APPS. Tiles show just an icon, a name and their Alt+letter. SETTINGS > STYLE > HOME TILES picks GLOW, TWO-TONE or NEON.
- **App toggles.** SYSTEM is now SETTINGS, with an APPLICATIONS tab and a switch per optional app (WEATHER, PLAYER, NET TOOLS). A switched-off app is greyed in APPS, runs nothing in the background and ignores its shortcut. Every app you have today stays on after the update.
- **WIRELESS TOOLS.** A new row in APPLICATIONS. NET TOOLS (the default) is the deck's own Wi-Fi put to work: a scan of the networks around you, with a filter for the flagged ones and a way to mark yours, plus ping, DNS, traceroute, port check, LAN devices and a speed test, under APPS and Alt+R. SENTINEL is the suite for the RF Sentinel carrier board on the rear M5-Bus (a Stamp C5 with a 433 MHz radio, a 2.4 GHz radio and a GPS): Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the radio bands watched all the time, trackers that stay with you, wardriving, captures, a site survey, a jam watch, and the network tools inside it. Pick one, press RESTART, and the deck comes back with it. A deck that had RF WATCH switched on comes up with SENTINEL; without the board nothing on the rear bus is powered.
- **Warnings that land.** An alert (likely jamming, a deauth flood, a karma or beacon flood) shows as a red ALERT banner in any app, replaces a plain warning's banner, stays twenty seconds and wakes the screen. The status bar count turns red while an alert is unseen, and the screensaver clock says how many warnings are waiting and the latest one.
- **Shortcuts.** Alt+1..6 open the home tiles, Alt+A opens APPS, Alt+R NET TOOLS or Alt+X SENTINEL. Alt+H lists them all.

### Install
- Over the air: SETTINGS > UPDATE, or accept the popup.
- Over USB: the web flasher, or write the full image at 0x0.
