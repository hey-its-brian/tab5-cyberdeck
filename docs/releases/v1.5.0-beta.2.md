## Beta: wireless tools, warnings that land, and a release you can ship

What a v1.5.0-beta.1 deck already has stays as it is: the live panel, six fixed tiles (TERMINAL, NOTES, SETTINGS, CALC, FILES, APPS), app toggles and the three tile styles. This beta adds one setting and makes warnings reach you.

- **WIRELESS TOOLS.** SETTINGS > APPLICATIONS has a new row. NET TOOLS (the default) is the deck's own Wi-Fi put to work: a scan of the networks around you (with a filter for the flagged ones and a way to mark yours), ping, DNS, traceroute, port check, LAN devices and a speed test, as before under APPS and Alt+R. SENTINEL is the radio board suite for the RF Sentinel carrier board on the rear M5-Bus (a Stamp C5 with a 433 MHz radio, a 2.4 GHz radio and a GPS): Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the radio bands watched all the time, trackers that stay with you, wardriving, captures, and the network tools inside it. Pick one, press RESTART, and the deck comes back with it. A deck that had RF WATCH switched on comes up with SENTINEL; every other deck stays on NET TOOLS, and without the board nothing on the rear bus is powered. The one thing NET TOOLS lacks from the old SCAN is the channel chart, which needs the board.
- **Warnings land.** An alert (likely jamming, a deauth flood, a karma or beacon flood) now shows as a red ALERT banner in any app: it replaces a plain warning's banner, stays twenty seconds, and wakes the screen from the saver or from sleep. The status bar count turns red while an alert is unseen. The screensaver clock says how many warnings are waiting and the latest one.
- **SENTINEL, finished from the usability review.** BACK says where it goes. A wardrive carries on through the board dropping off the link; BACK and the alert line ask before ending it (leaving SENTINEL for another app still ends it); its one big button starts and ends it. A capture names its file while it records and when it stops. Retuning or leaving TRANSMITTERS asks before its lists are lost; the survey's stop button is named. Anything running or being measured is in a blue that no accent theme uses, so NOMAD and MILITECH cannot be taken for warn or ok. Your own Bluetooth devices are left out of a drive.

### Install
- Over the air: SETTINGS > UPDATE (BETA on), or accept the popup.
